About us

TrendSwiss GmbH

Stylish comfort for every day – your experts in premium footwear

Welcome to TrendSwiss GmbH!

As a modern Swiss company, we are dedicated to a clear mission: bringing high-quality walking and standing comfort directly to you. As the official general distributor for internationally leading manufacturers, we bridge traditional craftsmanship, modern design and contemporary ergonomics.

Our philosophy: quality you can feel

Much of our lives is spent on our feet. Whether in a demanding workday, at home or in leisure time – our feet deserve the best possible support. At TrendSwiss we select our collections according to the strictest criteria. We consistently rely on premium natural materials (such as fine genuine leather) and meticulous workmanship that guarantees durability and an optimal wearing experience.

Our range: variety meets function

With a carefully curated and versatile range, we offer the right answer to different needs. From classic clogs for professional use in practices, pharmacies or care settings to elegant sandals and comfortable slippers for the whole family: our products combine an anatomically shaped footbed with modern design. They fit harmoniously into every situation in life and promote daily wellbeing with every step.

Your reliable Swiss partner

TrendSwiss GmbH sees itself not only as a retailer, but as a long-term partner. Thanks to our direct connection to manufacturing facilities, we can act flexibly, ensure the highest quality standards and always offer you an attractive price-performance ratio.

Whether you are a trade customer looking to enrich your range with our quality products or a private customer seeking the perfect companion for everyday life – the customer and first-class service are always our priority.

Why TrendSwiss?

  • Exclusive collections: Carefully selected models from established international producers.
  • Ergonomic design: Anatomically considered fits for fatigue-free standing and walking in everyday life.
  • Natural materials: High-quality leather and breathable components for a pleasant foot climate.
  • Swiss reliability: Competent service, straightforward handling and genuine customer proximity.

Take the path to success with us and experience comfort anew.
